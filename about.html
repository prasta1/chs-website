About us

Our mission

Our mission is the caring for our collection, interpreting the historical, cultural economic and technological contexts of these artifacts for a range of audiences. In doing so, we enrich the social and economic fabric of the community.

The Warner Lodge

Our home is the historic Warner Lodge, a 19th century Masonic lodge with a very well preserved Victorian-era lodge room that contains an extensive collection of Masonic artifacts. In addition we have rotating displays pertinent to our local history. The Warner Lodge is open by appointment only, or you may come to one of our monthly educational programs hosted on the 2nd Wednesdays of the month at 7 pm.
