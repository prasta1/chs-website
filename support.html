Support the Society

The Cambridge Historical Society relies on many forms of support to carry out our mission. You can help preserve Cambridge’s rich history now with your contribution of time, talent or money.

When you become a member you join a diverse group of people supporting our efforts to preserve, protect and share our rich heritage. Your participation helps ensure that Cambridge’s historic artifacts are preserved for future generations, and that the public and our schoolchildren learn about their past.

Other ways to help

Make a donation

Gifts of any size go directly to caring for the collection and keeping our programs free. Send a check payable to Cambridge Historical Society, PO Box 16, Jeffersonville, VT 05464.

Email us →

Donate an item

Photographs, documents, tools, textiles, ledgers — if it tells a Cambridge story, we’d like to hear about it. Write to us with a short description and we’ll be in touch about whether it fits the collection.

Ready to give something? The deed of gift form records the transfer.

Just come along

You do not need to be a member to attend any of our meetings or events. Find a subject that interests you, come to the meeting, introduce yourself, and you’ll be warmly greeted. Each meeting has refreshments catered by one of our members — great cooks all.
